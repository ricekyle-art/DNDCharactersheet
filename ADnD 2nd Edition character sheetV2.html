Weapon Specialization

Fighters can specialize in specific weapons for combat bonuses:

  • Proficient: No penalty
  • Specialized: +1 to hit, +2 damage, extra attacks (costs 2 slots)
  • Double Specialized: +3 to hit, +3 damage, more attacks (costs 3 additional slots, level 5+ only)

Track specialization in your weapon proficiency slots above.
